# Readings for names shown in kanji (device sweep 2026-11-26)

The English device sweep showed about 50 on-screen names whose Korean and English fall back to the Japanese text (no reading). Two research passes looked for readings. Only readings with at least one good source (medium) were applied; readings that rest only on the ordinary reading of the words were not, and stay in the round-6 backlog.
